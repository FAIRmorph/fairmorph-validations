The CS-PHOC original CSV files are **not checked in here**; they are obtained from the pinned upstream source during validation. Their original CC0 declaration remains applicable. The baseline uses upstream Git object SHA-1 identifiers; the FAIRmorph Engine independently computes SHA-256 of imported file bytes. These two identifiers must never be substituted for one another.

CC0 does not impose an attribution condition, but **scientific citation of the original dataset and publication is strongly encouraged**. Publication text, figures and other third-party content may have separate terms; check the source's specific license before copying such material.

## Generated evidence

RUN identifiers, checksums, pipeline revisions, summaries and subsequently approved scientific contracts document the validation procedure; generation of evidence does not grant new rights to the original data or imply scientific approval by its authors. Clearly distinguish source observations from independently verified output and from curatorial approvals.

For future datasets, record source URI, version/commit, checksums, license, citation and any redistribution restrictions in a dataset-specific notice. Do not assume that other datasets are CC0.
